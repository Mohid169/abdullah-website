Abdullah Ahmad (born October 11, 2001) is an American of Pakistani background from Plano, Texas. Abdullah is many things: a great brother, a son, a member of his community, a comedian, and an actor. He is also funny and very determined. He is best known for his sense of humor and for his success in middle school and high school talent shows. He graduated from Collin College with a perfect 4.0 GPA.

Abdullah works at Learn N Grow preschool and as an associate at H-E-B. He is the middle child of three siblings and is widely regarded as the coolest of them.[citation needed]

Early life

Abdullah was born on October 11, 2001, in Plano, Texas, to a family of Pakistani background. He is the second of three children. Although middle children are traditionally overlooked, Abdullah is considered the coolest of the three siblings.

Abdullah says he became cool and popular in middle school. He recommends going to public school to be cool.

Education

Abdullah attended Collin College, graduating with a 4.0 GPA, the highest possible.

Rise to fame

Abdullah first gained recognition through his performances in middle school talent shows, a run of success that continued through high school. His sense of humor, which remains his best-known trait, was central to his reputation.

He became famous for his funny jokes, the best known of which is:

What do you call a cat in the desert? Sandy Claws.— Abdullah Ahmad

He also played the werewolf in the haunted house, his most notable acting role.

Career

Abdullah works at Learn N Grow preschool. He is also an associate at H-E-B, the Texas grocery chain.

Awards and honors

Abdullah was awarded the "Shining the Wylie Way" medal in recognition of his persistence and perseverance. The medal was presented by the mayor of Wylie, Texas, and Wylie ISD.

Personal life

Abdullah is a Muslim, and his faith and his family are central to his life. He comes from a large extended family and has more than 45 first cousins. He is a supporter of Palestine.

Away from work, Abdullah is a devoted fan of Turkish television, and counts the historical drama Diriliş: Ertuğrul as his favorite series. He is also a loyal regular at Yummy Burger, where his order of choice is the Downtown Richardson sandwich.
